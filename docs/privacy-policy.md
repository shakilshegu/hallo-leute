# Privacy Policy – Poppylo

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026

Poppylo ("the app", "we") helps you learn German vocabulary. This policy
explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

**In short:** we store your learning progress in your account so it is never
lost and works on any phone. We do not sell your data, show ads or track you
across other apps.

## What we collect

**Your account**

- When you first open the app, it creates an anonymous **guest account** (a
  random ID) so your progress can be saved. No name or email is needed for
  this.
- If you choose **Save your progress**, we also store the **email address** you
  enter, or the email address and name from your **Google account** if you use
  Google sign-in.

**What you tell us during setup**

- Your first name (used to greet you), why you are learning German, your
  level and your daily goal.

**Your learning activity**

- Which words you have seen and learned, how often you answered "Got it" or
  "Again", when each word is next due for review, and the date and time of
  each answer.

We do **not** collect your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera
data, or any advertising ID.

## Why we use it

- To save your progress and sync it between your phones.
- To schedule reviews (spaced repetition) and show your streak and statistics.
- To improve the app and its word lists. For example, we look at which words
  learners find hardest. For this we study **combined statistics**, not
  individual people.

## Where it is stored and who can see it

- Your data is stored with **Supabase**, our database provider, in
  **Mumbai, India**. It is sent over encrypted
  connections (HTTPS).
- Each account can only access its own data. Our small team can see learner
  data in order to run and improve the app.
- If you use **Google sign-in**, Google confirms your identity to us. Sign-in
  codes are sent by our email provider, **Resend**.
- We **never sell** your data and never share it with advertisers.

## How long we keep it

- We keep your data while your account exists.
- Guest accounts that are not used for 12 months may be deleted.

## Deleting your data

- **In the app:** Profile → **Delete account**. This permanently deletes your
  account and all your learning data from our servers and your phone.
- **Without the app:** email **[CONTACT EMAIL]** from your account's email
  address and we will delete it within 30 days.
- Uninstalling the app removes the copy on your phone. A guest account's data
  stays on our servers until it is deleted as described above.

## Children

The app is intended for teenagers and adults (13+). It is not directed at
children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe
a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Third-party content

The vocabulary and example sentences come from open datasets: Wiktionary,
Tatoeba and FrequencyWords. They are bundled inside the app, and using them
sends no data to those projects.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the effective
date above, and mention significant changes in the app.
